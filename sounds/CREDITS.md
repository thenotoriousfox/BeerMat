# Default sounds

Laid out as a sound pack (`general/` for each moment, `buzzers/<pack>/`), as is a laptop's own `soundpack/`.

Every file in this folder is in the public domain except the two Music buzzers: CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), or, for the Famous Moments, recordings made by the US government,
which have no copyright. The Music buzzers are Kevin MacLeod's, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which needs a credit people can find: it's in the desk (Settings →
Credits) and at https://beermatquiz.co.uk/credits. Keep both in step with this folder. Files were converted to mono MP3, and the applause
sound was shortened with a fade.
