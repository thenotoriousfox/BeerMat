                          Teams & recovery

                          Issue a new recovery code when a team loses access. This revokes the old code. Disabled teams cannot answer and are hidden from the public leaderboard; their history is kept.

                          Returning teams

                          Teams that have played here, saved when a quiz finishes. Read a team its PIN: on the phone they open “Played here before, or lost your team? Get it back” and type it, and the team is back with its name, photo and buzzer sound.

                            BeerMat Free makes and rehearses whole quizzes, with simulated teams and real phones testing the connection. BeerMat Pro, for venues, hosts them live with real teams.                                           Spotify (optional)

                                          For private quizzes only. Spotify's terms cover personal listening, so don't use it to play music to a pub audience: use uploaded files and the venue's music licence there. Needs internet on this laptop (for example hotspot.py --keep-wifi) and Spotify Premium. Phones never use Spotify.

                                          One-time setup
                                          1. Sign in at developer.spotify.com/dashboard and create an app, ticking Web API.
                                          2. Add this exact Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:3000/spotify/callback
                                          3. Copy the app's Client ID here and press Connect Spotify. Open the Spotify app on this laptop and play any song once so it shows up as a player.

                                          Not connected

                                          The desk player plays through this laptop's speakers or HDMI. Keep this tab open while music rounds run. It needs Chrome, Firefox or Edge; if it won't start, open the Spotify app instead and pick it below.

                                          Keyboard shortcuts

                                          Keys: N next · C close · P pause/resume · R reveal · 1 correct · 2 wrong · 3 pass · A agree countdown · B call bingo number · W spin wheel · T pause/resume countdown · S send the question (with Send Question on), otherwise scores/positions. A Stream Deck or presenter remote set to send these keys works too.
